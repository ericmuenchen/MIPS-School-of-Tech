Course Description

PC Pro B is the second semester of the CompTIA A+ pathway and continues directly where PC Pro A left off. Last semester you built and configured the machine itself, its hardware, operating systems, and storage. This semester you learn everything that keeps a real system running, connected, and safe: managing users and updates, controlling files and permissions, wiring up networks and devices, and locking systems down against threats. You'll keep working in TestOut LabSim's simulated environment, reading, watching demonstrations, and practicing hands-on, then finish the year ready to sit for certification.

The term moves outward from the single machine to the whole environment. You'll start with System Management (Active Directory, remote access, updates, and recovery) and File Management (permissions and encryption), then connect the world with Peripheral Devices, Networking, Mobile Devices, and Printing. You'll close with Security and a set of Capstone Exercises that pull the whole year together and prepare you for the TestOut PC Pro and CompTIA A+ exams.

How This Course Works

This course is fully asynchronous: there are no required live sessions, so you can do the work when it fits your schedule. Announcements and feedback come through email and Google Classroom, and the lessons, labs, and quizzes themselves live in TestOut LabSim. I'll set weekly checkpoints to help you stay on pace across the eight sections we cover this semester.

Optional weekly meetup

Each week I host an optional 30-minute meeting where you can share projects, connect with classmates, play games, and discuss emerging technologies. It's not required, but it's a fun way to stay connected to the class.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this semester you'll be able to manage users and files, connect a system to networks, devices, and printers, and secure it against real threats, the day-to-day work of a professional IT technician, and you'll be ready to earn your certification.

Manage the system
Run Active Directory, users and groups, updates, backups, files, and permissions.
Connect everything
Wire up networks, peripherals, mobile devices, and printers, and troubleshoot them.
Secure and certify
Apply security controls, remove malware, and prep for the A+ and PC Pro exams.
  • Set up Active Directory domains, user accounts, and groups, and configure remote access with RDP and VPN
  • Manage Windows Updates, restore points, and backups, and recover from boot errors
  • Control NTFS and share permissions and inheritance, and encrypt drives with BitLocker
  • Configure displays and peripheral drivers, and troubleshoot display and device failures
  • Apply TCP/IP addressing (IPv4/IPv6, static, DHCP, DNS, APIPA) and configure SOHO routers, switches, firewalls, and Wi-Fi
  • Troubleshoot connectivity with ping, traceroute, and ipconfig
  • Service laptops and mobile devices, and maintain and troubleshoot laser, inkjet, thermal, impact, and 3D printers
  • Apply password, authentication, and physical/logical security controls, remove malware, and prepare for the TestOut PC Pro and CompTIA A+ (Core 1 & Core 2) exams

Grading

Your grade is your total score across assignments, projects, and quizzes. Each week you'll get a progress report showing two numbers: your overall grade, which reflects only the work you've turned in so far, and your relative grade, which also counts anything not yet submitted as a zero — in other words, what your grade would be if the semester ended today.

Course Policies

Pacing & deadlines

Weekly targets keep your progress steady, but you're welcome to work ahead. If you fall behind, reach out early and we can build a plan to help you catch up before the semester ends.

Late work

Because this course is fully asynchronous, there is no penalty for late work. The weekly schedule is a pacing guide to help you stay on track over the semester, not a set of hard deadlines. Staying close to the pace keeps the workload manageable and makes sure you finish every project before the term ends.

Communication

Email me anytime at muenchen@miprepschool.org or send me a Google chat. I typically respond within one school day.

AI Policy

The goal is for you to learn to think like an IT technician, not to submit answers you can't explain.

AI tools can be great tutors, and IT professionals use AI as a standard part of their workflow. But you are just at the beginning of your IT education, and the CompTIA A+ exam expects you to know this material yourself. If AI does all the work and thinking for you, learning never happens.

Here is how to acceptably use AI in this course:

  • Use AI to explain a concept, decode an error message, or ask "why doesn't this work?" — not to answer quiz or lab questions for you.
  • You should be able to explain every answer you submit. If it looks like answers were copied without understanding, I will ask to meet with you so you can walk me through your reasoning.
  • When in doubt, ask me first. I would much rather help you than have you turn in work you cannot explain.
